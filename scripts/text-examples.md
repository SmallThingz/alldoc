All text, code, contacts, calendar entries, mail, and artwork in
`app/src/main/assets/examples/text` were authored for AllDoc. They contain no
third-party document content. `example.invalid` names and addresses are fictional.

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/generate-text-examples.py`. Host-only encoders:
FFmpeg (JPEG/WebP/GIF/BMP/TIFF), libheif `heif-enc` (HEIC/HEIF), and libavif
`avifenc` (AVIF). PNG, WBMP, and flattened RGB PSD/PSB are written directly from
the same original chart. No generator or encoder is an APK dependency.

Extensions that name the same format deliberately share content: JPEG aliases,
TIFF aliases, HEIF aliases, source-language aliases, and certificate containers.
PSD and PSB use their respective versioned headers and a saved merged composite.
The image samples demonstrate previews, not OCR or editable layers. HEIF/AVIF
availability depends on the Android device's decoder.

The self-signed RSA certificate names AllDoc Examples / example.invalid and is
valid from 2026 to 2036. Only its public certificate is retained; the randomly
generated private key was never serialized and was discarded. PKCS#7 samples
contain that certificate, without signatures or private keys. These are display
examples, not trusted certificates.

Markup, diagrams, stylesheets, and program samples follow the reader's actual
source-text capabilities. They are not executed. SVG is a vector preview with
source-markup export. The longer plain-text journal exercises scrolling; image
formats represent a single image by design.
